Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This privacy policy applies to all mobile apps and games published by us on Google Play and other app stores ("the apps"). It explains what happens to your information when you use them.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store on a server, or sell any personal information. The apps have no accounts or sign-in, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.

Data saved on your device

Your progress and settings (for example levels, scores, coins, unlocked items and sound settings) are saved only on your own device. This data never leaves your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

The apps do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, photos or files. Most of them work fully offline.

Notifications

Some apps may offer reminders (for example a daily reward). These are scheduled on your device itself; nothing is sent to us or to any server. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Advertising

Some apps may show ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads and to measure and protect them, Google may collect and use information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • how you interact with ads

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads). Apps without ads do not share any data with ad providers.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

App store services

An app may ask whether you would like to rate it. This uses the standard Google Play or App Store rating window; any review you write is handled by that store under its own privacy policy.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. In apps that are suitable for children, ads (where shown) are limited to content suitable for children and are not personalised for them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page and the date above will be updated.
